# D85, the onset of a lost branch (branch d85-onset)

Branch `d85-onset` from v7-merge `136667a` (D83 and D84, the shadowing selection included). This note is the merge input for the D85 entry of `docs/DECISIONS.md` (part "onset"); it does not replace it. Every choice below was made on code, physics, the sources and validation-style data; no test reading was taken.

## Why

A research pass on validation data (under D84) measured that an antenna fault and airframe shadowing reach only about 66% and 81% recall within the 0.24-0.42 s fusion window from steady-state RF cues: both lose one receive branch, and what tells them apart in the steady state (the hidden antenna's lost line of sight, its K-factor near -16 dB) is noisy over a few cycles. The physical difference the receiver can observe is the onset. A bank is entered over a finite roll-in, so shadowing grows as the roll passes the rolls of Sun et al.'s events, while an open connector steps in within one frame. Under D84 both were steps in the model: the fault was on from the run's first frame, and shadowing switched its whole depth on in the frame the attitude first hid the antenna. The research pass's Monte Carlo on the measured per-cycle spreads (fault branch 1.42 dB, hidden branch 3.21 dB, clean offset 1.35 dB) separated a step from a linear ramp at 86-99% for depths of 9 dB and more and 91-99.8% from 12 dB, for ramps of 0.25 s and longer, 5-50 cycles after the alarm (scratch `weak_v2/wv/wv_task3.m`, log `j309c_task3`).

## Decision

### N1. The roll-in and the shadowing ramp (link)

- **Roll-in** (`turn_attitude.m`, new). A flight that rolls into its turn flies level until `t_in`, then rolls at a constant rate `w` up to the turn's bank `b` (a linear roll-in), and its heading rate follows the roll as a coordinated turn, `g tan(roll) / v = yaw tan(roll) / tan(b)` (`bank_angle.m`), so the heading changes by `yaw / tan(b) (-ln cos(w u)) / w` after `u` seconds of the roll-in and at the turn's rate once the bank is reached. The GCS direction, the roll and the heading change of every frame come from it: the channel's antenna gain toward the GCS (and its flight-start value in 'GCS'), the LoS Doppler, the steering vectors and the diffuse part's centre, every interferer's direction and lever-arm phase, the in-band cap's weakest antenna (`sig_weak_db.m`) and the hidden antenna. The 'Att' block grows from 7 to 10 values: the roll-in's start [s], its rate [deg/s] and the roll at which the shadowing event started (0 0 0: a flight that holds its bank, as before; every arithmetic path of such a flight is D84's, the heading change `yaw t` kept as one term).
- **Roll rate** (`roll_rate.m`, new; first draw of a new seed stream 'roll', offset 19). Uniform from the measured roll-in of a small UAV, 30 deg in about 1.6 s = 18.75 deg/s (Allen & Lin, NASA TM-2007-214611, Fig. 12, digitised: the soaring entry; its steepest windows 31.7 deg/s over 0.5 s and 28.8 deg/s over 1 s lie below Gross's), up to the largest rate measured over a window as long as the roll-in itself (Gross et al. 2016, Fig. 4, digitised: 45.4 deg/s over 0.5 s, 35.5 deg/s over 1 s): a roll-in of `T = b / w` seconds may reach 45.4 deg/s when `T <= 0.5 s`, 35.5 deg/s when `T >= 1 s`, linearly in `T` between the two windows (a derivation: `w = (a + sqrt(a^2 - 4 s b)) / 2` with `s = 19.8 deg/s^2`, `a = 55.3 deg/s`). So the most severe measured rate (the most step-like ramp) bounds every roll-in and none exceeds a measured one. `p.roll_rate_lo`, `p.roll_rate_win`.
- **The ramp** (`shadow_frame.m`, new). The hidden antenna is `shadow_hidden.m`'s geometry at the frame's attitude (the GCS above the belly's horizon, more than 30 deg off the heading, the far end of the array, not below 8 m/s), and the level's roll band (`shadow_roll_band.m`, Sun et al.'s C-band fit) is checked on the turn's bank: the event's largest roll, which Sun et al. pair with its median loss. During the roll-in the antenna loses the share `f(x) = (3x - x^3) / 2` of the level, the rising half of Sun et al.'s shape of the loss within an event (Part IV, Sec. IV-C, eq. 8: a cubic spline through (0, 0), (0.5, 1), (1, 0) of the event's duration), with `x` the progress of the roll from the roll at which the airframe enters the line of sight (the GCS on the belly's horizon, or the roll at which the heading criterion came true when that is later: `r_s`, kept in 'Att') to the bank. Its K-factor moves in dB from the antenna's own to the shadow's (-16 dB) by the same share. In a turn that holds its bank `x = 1`: the whole depth and K, D84 exactly (5 000 random attitudes: `shadow_frame` equals `shadow_hidden` with the share 1).
- **What counts** (`p.shadow_evt_db` = 5 dB). The event counts as on the air (the threat block's `actc`, the labels, `d.hidden`, the selections) while the loss reaches 5 dB: Sun et al.'s third criterion to declare an event (Sec. III, at least 5 dB over the log-distance path loss). Below it a frame is the clean link's, as a WLAN frame without a packet: its one-antenna loss is within the airframe's own spread between antennas (D84 T-F4: 4.9 dB at p90).
- **Placing the onset** (`flight_draws.m`). A flight rolls in when the data ask for an onset: `p.roll_in_frames` (the detector's sub-runs; the third 'roll' draw picks the frame, as path loss draws its step) places the moment the loss first reaches 5 dB at the build's level; `geo.event_frame` (the pools) places the moment the airframe enters the line of sight, the same for every level, so the clean link of the same flight flies the same timeline. The timeline is scanned every millisecond over the roll-in from the drawn GCS direction (the direction at the roll-in's start), and the roll-in starts that long before the target, 0.1-0.9 of a cycle before the frame (second draw) so that the frame is the first one counted. The event must start during the roll-in: a flight whose event would start only in the held turn (the GCS turning into the region, a step in this model) or never is no shadowing flight there (`d.hidden` 0), and every selection skips it (`hides_antenna.m`, unchanged code).
- **Antenna fault** (`build_threat_model.m`). The connector opens at `p.fault_onset_frames` (drawn per run over a range, `af_on`, after every other draw of the threat stream; with the pools' `[1 1]` nothing is drawn, so the pools' fault runs are D84's): frames before it carry no threat, from it the faulty antenna's whole field is attenuated, a step within one frame (D83's connector ahead of the receiver unchanged).
- **Stated, not measured.** No source measures the loss of airframe shadowing against time on a small UAV: tying Sun et al.'s shape to the roll-in (its peak at the bank), the K-factor following the same shape, the linear roll-in at a constant rate and the rate's uniform draw are assumptions; the rate bounds and the 5 dB criterion are measured or stated values of the sources. Only the flights whose shadowing onset the data place roll in; a turn entered without shadowing is not modelled (clean flights keep their held bank), so the roll-in's common change of the antenna gain toward the GCS (under 2.5 dB at a 30 deg bank) appears only on shadowing flights and their pool references. A shadowing event that begins inside a held turn, as the GCS turns into the region, has no onset model (it would be a step) and is not flown.

### N2. Onsets inside the data

- **Detector dataset** (`run_dataset_sweep.m`; the second test set FRESH_TEST and B4/B4s through `eval_unseen_snr.m`, `eval_unseen_severity.m`, `detector_block.m` and `hiding_flight.m` follow the same recipe): `p.fault_onset_frames = p.roll_in_frames = p.pl_onset_det` (frames 2-11 of 20, as the path-loss step). The connector opens at the drawn frame; the shadowing flight's loss reaches 5 dB at it. Frames before the onset are 'none' through `threat_active.m` (`act` 0), unchanged code; from the onset on a fault frame and a counted shadowing frame keep their class (a lost branch stays visible in the steady state, unlike the path-loss step); `fusion_target.m` keeps a lost branch's labels, as before. The two cells fly `round(N_SUB F_SUB / 14.5)` = 8 sub-runs (14.5 = the mean frames from an onset in 2-11), going round the folds as the extra sub-runs of path loss, so their labelled frames match the other threats'. The dataset records each sub-run's roll-in (`dataset.roll_in`).
- **Pools** (`build_policy_pools.m`, `build_check_pools.m`, `pool_cell.m`, `link_env.m`; `decision_config.m` `C.event_frame` 11, `C.event_run` 38, `C.event_count` 30). The shadowing groups' flights (D84's own geometries of every cell holding airframe shadowing, and the clean link's reference flights on them) roll in so that the airframe enters the line of sight just before frame 11 of a 38-frame run (`geo.frames`, `geo.event`; the cap holds over the 38 frames). An episode on such a flight starts at `k0 = 11 - 1 - onset`, so its onset cycle (3-10) reads frame 11: the cycles before it are the same flight's clean link before the event (the roll-in under way), those after it the ramp, frame by frame; 11 - 3 + 30 = 38 frames hold the latest onset's lead-in and the earliest one's 27 cycles, and beyond the run's end (the 3D console's endless episodes) the episode cycles over the run's last 10 frames. The per-flight tables (rewards, restored, healthy, recoverable, the clean reference of the same flight, `PP.mber`) read such a run from frame 11 on (`event_rows.m`, link_env's `span`). The selection keeps a candidate only when its event starts during the roll-in and its lowest level's loss counts by frame 30 (inside the shortest span after an onset, 20 cycles), so every kept flight has the threat on the air in every episode (`K.on`). The antenna fault's pool runs keep the connector open from their first frame: the episode's onset joins the clean frames and the fault's, a step. `PP.event_frame` and `CK.event_frame` (per split, NaN where none) are new fields (`rekey_pools.m` knows it); `PP.cov.roll_in`; `MODEL_TAG` 'v7-D85'.

### N3. The onset-shape cue (`code/detection/onset_shape.m`, new)

- **Statistic.** From the receiver's per-antenna channel gains (`gain_ant`, the receiver's own estimates) over a window, the branch whose offset from the median of the others (Willgert's eq. 5, as `off_w`) is lowest over the last 3 cycles is fitted by least squares with a level before an onset cycle `tau` and a fall of depth `d` spread linearly over `R` cycles, `tau = 2..n`, `R = 1..2n` (a linear ramp is the neutral shape, not the simulator's own); `os_rate = 1 / R` of the best fit (1: a step) and `os_glr = (SSE_step - SSE_ramp) / s^2`, the best step against the best ramp longer than one cycle in units of the residual variance (at least (0.5 dB)^2): -2 log of their likelihood ratio under Gaussian errors, positive where a ramp fits better. A window whose best step is shallower than 5 dB carries 0 0 (no branch falls there: Heath's diversity-imbalance indicator fires at a branch difference above 5 dB; Sun et al.'s event needs 5 dB).
- **(a) Fusion evidence.** `temporal_evidence.m` appends `os_rate` and `os_glr` over its window (C + 11 columns); `fuse_classes.m` and the monitor's fusion build the same row, `select_fusion.m` refits on validation, and the agent's state grows by the two persistence columns (`policy_state_size.m` reads the names).
- **(b) Latched diagnosis** (`onset_shape('latch', ...)`, one compact block in `policy_monitor.m`). Per episode it holds the last 20 cycles of gains; a branch whose 3-cycle median offset falls 5 dB below the median of its earlier cycles (at least 3) starts an event; 3 cycles later the branch's offsets over the held history (the onset and the lead-in before it) are fitted as in (a) and the cause is latched: airframe shadowing when `os_glr` exceeds the threshold `FM.os_thr`, an antenna fault otherwise. The threshold is chosen on validation (`select_fusion.m`): the statistic at the latch's diagnosis of every validation sub-run of the two threats (`onset_shape('events')`), the threshold that reads the two causes right in the largest mean share over a grid of 0.25 from -8 to 8, the one nearest 0 among equals (`onset_shape('choose')`); 0 (the model that fits better) where the fusion carries none. A threshold below 0 is expected: a step fits a sharp fall clearly better than any ramp (median statistic -6 to -28 for faults of 9-31 dB on the study's data below), while a slow ramp under the hidden branch's fading fits about as well as a step (median -0 to +5). While latched, a fused class of antenna_fault or airframe_shadowing reads the latched cause (the two classes' probability goes to it, so the agent's probabilities and the class agree); the latch resets once the branch's drop has stayed below 5 dB for `C.heal` = 5 cycles. Every other class, and a fused 'none', is left as it is: the latch never raises an alarm, it only chooses between the two branch classes, which the rule answers alike.

### N4. Readings beside KPI 1

- `eval_detector.m`: for the antenna fault and airframe shadowing, recall with the onset inside the fusion window (the frames up to N - 1 cycles after the sub-run's first frame of the class) and after it, by the fused class and by the monitor's class with the latched cause (`onset_shape('sequence', ...)` over each test sub-run), and the share read as the other branch class (`metrics.onset_cue`, `pred.y_latched`; `measure_all_kpis.m` prints it beside KPI 1).
- `det_frames.m`, `edge_map.m`: DET reads the monitor's class (the fused class and the latched cause); an antenna fault, alone or in a combination, is now read after the same flight's clean link as path loss is (its step between them), a rolling-in shadowing flight from its own lead-in; a new block of the edge map's report gives the fault's and the shadowing's DET with the onset in the window and after it, by the monitor's class and by the fused class alone (`EDGE_NUM.onset_cue`).
- The onset's shape is read at the event's start (fusion evidence while the onset lies in the window); the latched cause carries it after the window has passed.

## Open risks

1. **Only shadowing flights roll in.** Clean flights and every other threat keep their held bank, so the roll-in's common change of the antenna gain toward the GCS (under 2.5 dB at a 30 deg bank) and its heading change appear only around shadowing events and their pool references. The onset statistic reads a branch's offset from the others, where a common change cancels, but the per-frame network and the link features see the common level: a turn entered without shadowing is a physical event the data do not hold.
2. **Shadowing that begins inside a held turn** (the GCS turning into the shadowed region at the turn rate) is not flown: the model has no onset shape for it (it would be a step), so such an event, if it occurs, may read as a fault at its onset and the latch would hold that cause. The data's shadowing onsets are all roll-ins.
3. **The latch's threshold** is chosen on the validation sub-runs (select_fusion.m); with the dataset's 20-frame sub-runs and onsets at frames 2-11 a fault or shadowing sub-run decides only when 6 cycles of history precede the detection (a late onset or a slow ramp may not decide within the sub-run), so the choice rests on fewer events than sub-runs (the smoke chose -1.75 from 4 + 2 events; the study's folds chose -1.25 to -1.75 from about 230 events).
4. **Faults at the ends of the range:** at 5 dB the fault is within the airframe's own spread between antennas and the statistic reads it as a ramp as often as not (median -1.1); at 31 dB the faulty branch's channel estimate lies near the thermal floor and fluctuates, and the latch lowered its recall from 92% to 86% in the study. Both stay readable at the branch level.
5. **The pools' selection** keeps a shadowing flight only when its lowest level's loss counts by frame 30: at 8 dB this favours fast roll-ins and short spans from the airframe's entry to the bank (the readings are conditioned on such a flight, as D84's on a hiding flight).
6. **The edge map's DET of an antenna fault** now reads the fault after the same flight's clean link: the fusion windows of its first frames hold the clean lead-in, which lowers DET there against D84's reading (the onset cue and the latch are meant to carry it); the verdicts of the fault cells move.
7. **Onset latency of shadowing** (evaluate_policies.m, beside KPI 1) counts from the episode's onset, the airframe's entry into the line of sight, so it includes the ramp's frames below 5 dB.
8. **Merges.** The agent's state grows by 2 evidence columns per cycle (8 inputs at C.hist 4) through `temporal_evidence('names')`, and the monitor's mem gains `os`; the idle-RSSI work on `policy_monitor.m` (d85-obs) adds to the same observation, and the receiver work (d84-rx) edits other parts of `build_threat_model.m`: the attitude lines here are the channel's `tilt`/`dly`/`sel`/`shadow_code` and the threat block's first line, shadowing and fault cases and interferer direction lines.
9. **test_rate_reduce_chip_level** fails on this branch with the numbers d84_shadow.md reported for its base (mean image level under rate_reduce 0.93 dB below no_action with the 10 dB jammer, tolerance 0.5 dB): a receiver-side referral issue outside this change (see Results).
