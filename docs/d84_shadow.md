# D84, airframe shadowing on flights that hide an antenna (branch d84-shadow)

Branch `d84-shadow` from v7-merge `d299036` (D83 and the three D84 fix groups merged). Input: the open risk of group A (`docs/d84_link.md`, A2 and "Open risks"): after A2 airframe shadowing hides an antenna only in line-of-sight geometry, at the rolls Sun et al.'s C-band fit pairs with each level, and not below 8 m/s, while the decision-layer pools flew shared geometries for every cell. This note is the merge input for the D84 entry of `docs/DECISIONS.md`; it does not replace it.

## Why

`run_dataset_sweep.m` (A5) already selects, before simulating, flights whose attitude hides an antenna in some frame of the sub-run. The decision-layer pools (`build_policy_pools.m`, `build_check_pools.m`) flew the shared geometries of every split for every cell (common random numbers, D74), so a shadowing cell had the threat on the air only on the flights whose attitude happened to hide an antenna at its level: 5.6-24.7% of them (A2's table, `shadow_hit_share.m`), about 4-18 of a point's 72. `link_env.m` K.on correctly drops the others as no-threat episodes, so the edge map's shadowing points ended UNDETERMINED (n below N_MIN = 36), KPI 4 and 5 rested on a few episodes per level, the class table (`policy_table.m`) averaged the shadowing class's rewards mostly over flights where nothing was hidden, and the combinations with airframe shadowing carried the shadowing component on a minority of their flights. B4 and B4s (`eval_unseen_snr.m`, `eval_unseen_severity.m`) drew their block seeds the same blind way.

## Decision

### Selection (what a shadowing cell flies)
- A cell holding airframe shadowing, alone or in a combination, flies geometries of its own in every pool split. Each flight is the first candidate, in the flight's strata of its split's design, whose own draws let the airframe hide an antenna at the cell's level in some frame of the 20-frame run: `hides_antenna.m`, `any(d.hidden > 0)` of `flight_draws.m` with the draws `link_seed.m` gives the model (heading rate, bank, GCS direction and elevation at the Eb/N0's distance, the level's roll band, the 30 deg heading criterion, the speed floor). The same predicate now serves A5 (`run_dataset_sweep.m`, unchanged behaviour), the pools, the check pools, B4 and B4s.
- Selection decides only WHICH flights a shadowing cell flies. The flight, the threat and its level are simulated as before; a flight that hides no antenna is, under the shadowing build, exactly the clean link (A2), so leaving it out removes no shadowing physics. The model hides an antenna on every selected flight (checked in the pools after the simulation: `PP.shadow.on`, and in `test_shadow_selection` on the model itself).
- Levels flown at the same rolls hide an antenna on exactly the same flights (the depth enters `shadow_hidden.m` only through the band) and share them (`shadow_groups.m`): at the decision levels four groups, 8 dB (rolls 16.1-33.0 deg), 12 dB (16.1-49.0), 15.5 and 20 dB (16.1-57.9), 25 dB (32.0-57.9); a combination flies with the single threat of its level (12, 15.5, 20 dB), as every cell shares the shared geometries.
- Speeds: no source measures shadowing below `p.shadow_v_min` = 8 m/s (A2, Khawaja et al.), and a flight banks only as far as its coordinated turn, tan(bank) = w v / g (`bank_angle.m`), with the heading rate at most the measured 28.7 deg/s (Allen & Lin, `heading_rate.m`): below v = g tan(lo) / w_max no flight reaches the band's smallest roll lo (`shadow_speed_floor.m`, a physics derivation). The 25 dB level (lo 32.0 deg) can occur only from 12.24 m/s = 44.1 km/h; every other decision level from 28.8 km/h. Each group's candidates draw their speed over that range (uniform, from the seed's own speed stream).

### Layout (`pool_layout.m`): the pools' design kept
- Positions in a split: the shared geometries (1..NGEO), the combined threats' own (NCOMB), then per shadowing group its own: part 1 (the single threat's, and every cell's of the group in a split without combinations' own geometries), part 2 (its combinations' own). Every (cell, Eb/N0) keeps its per-split count: train 8, validation 6, test 12, second test 60, the combined threats 24 of their own in the second test (12 + 24 = 36 per point), as before.
- Edge speeds: the single threats keep 24 hover flights at every level and 12 at 161 km/h at the nominal level. Shadowing cannot hide at hover (no source below 8 m/s): the nominal shadowing level flies 12 selected flights at 161 km/h only, the other levels no edge-speed flights; the edge map's hover band for shadowing has no flight and the report states it as outside the sources (`shadow_note.m`).
- Designs: test and second test keep their nested Latin hypercubes (speed, altitude, K, first interferer direction, receive correlation), each group's design with its own permutation streams (LHS_SEED + 100 s + 10 (group + 1) + split, + 5 for its combinations' own; the check pools LHS_SEED + 100 s + group), the speed axis over the group's speed range; the selection keeps one flight per stratum (the candidate's own draws inside the flight's strata). Train and validation draw over the group's speed range without strata, the conditional population of the dataset (A5).
- The clean link flies the shadowing groups' flights too, so every shadowing episode's frames before the onset and its restoration reference come from the same flight (common random numbers, as for the combinations' own geometries). They are its reference only (`PP.ref_geo`, `CK.ref_geo`): the clean link's own flights (`PP.cell_geo`) are the shared and combined ones as before, and every clean-link statistic reads those only: the pools' clean reference (`clean_ref`, `PP.clean`: `own` in `build_policy_pools.m`; `CK.clean`), the drop threshold (`choose_drop_threshold.m`), the training and validation clean episodes (`link_env.m` K.geo), KPI 6 and the clean sets of `evaluate_policies.m`, the edge map's FA and LINK at every point, band and check point (`FT.ref`, `KF.ref`). A banked flight selected to hide an antenna is not a representative clean flight.
- Episodes: every builder runs each cell on the geometries it flies (`link_env.m` K.geo; `flight_triples.m`, `policy_episodes.m` with a list per cell): `evaluate_policies.m`, `train_dqn.m` (validation), the training draws (`link_env.m` reset without a geometry), `experiment_combo_generalization.m`, `experiment_unknown_threat.m`, the diagnostics and the 3D console (`v3d_engine.m`: a shadowing threat moves the episode to a flight of its own). With every cell on the same list the order is ndgrid's, so the other sets keep their draws.

### Seeds (disjoint from every other block and family)
- Pools and check pools: `shadow_pool_seed.m`, candidate j of (group g, Eb/N0 index s, seed block b): 10 000 000 + 100 000 (64 (g - 1) + 8 (s - 1) + position of b in [1 5 10 14 16 17 18 19]) + j, j = 1..99 999: 10 000 001-61 199 999, a range per (g, s, b), above every other flight seed (pools below 2 000 000, dataset 3.0/3.5 M, B4 4.0/4.5 M, B4s 5.0/5.5 M), below 2^26; a seed owns 64 streams (`seed_base.m`), the largest below 2^32. Candidates are tried once each, in order, a counter per (group, Eb/N0, block) running over the splits that share a block. The run ids continue the split's numbering (100 block + position; they number runs, no seed is derived from them).
- B4 and B4s: a shadowing block flies, in place of its block seed, the next candidate SEED0 + 50 000 + j (j < 50 000) whose flight hides an antenna (`hiding_flight.m`): 4 550 001-4 599 999 and 5 550 001-5 599 999, inside their ranges and above every block seed they use (B4 about 1 300 blocks at the full grid), so no seed is used twice; its speed is the candidate's own draw over the envelope (the dataset's law); the global speed stream still draws the block's speed, so every other block keeps its seed and speed.
- `test_seed_streams_disjoint` extended (the candidate ranges, the B4/B4s ranges, the permutation streams).

### Reporting
- `shadow_note.m`: the statement every shadowing reading carries, with the share beside it, per level and Eb/N0: the share of the design's flights from the group's speed up that hide an antenna (kept over candidates tried, `PP.shadow`), and of the whole envelope (times the fraction of the envelope the group is flown over; no slower flight hides one). It is printed in the edge map (`results/edge_map.txt` and the summary, `EM.shadow_note`), at the check points, in `evaluate_policies.m` (KPI 4, `KP.shadow_note`) and in `measure_all_kpis.m`'s KPI 4 details; README's threat model states it; B4 and B4s print the blocks kept over the candidates tried (`SHT`).

## Sources and derivations
- Sun et al., Air-Ground Channel Characterization for UAS, Part IV: Airframe Shadowing, IEEE TVT 66(9), 2017: Sec. III (event criteria), Table II (C-band fit, rolls 16.1-58.3 deg): the bands (A2, unchanged).
- Khawaja et al. 2019: the slowest measured flying UAV, 8 m/s (`p.shadow_v_min`, A2, unchanged).
- Allen & Lin 2007 (NASA TM, Fig. 12): the largest measured yaw rate of a small UAV, 28.7 deg/s (`p.yaw_rate_max`, unchanged); Gross et al. (Fig. 4): the largest bank, 57.9 deg (unchanged).
- Derivation: the coordinated turn, tan(bank) = w v / g (`bank_angle.m`, unchanged): the speed floor of a level, 9.81 tan(lo) / (28.7 pi / 180) m/s.
- No new physical constant. J_MAX, the candidate ranges and the permutation streams are bookkeeping.

## Stated, not backed by a source
- The shadowing readings hold conditioned on a flight whose attitude hides an antenna at the level; the share of such flights is stated beside them, not folded into the rates.
- The test and second-test designs of a shadowing group stratify the speed uniformly over the speeds the level can occur at (as the other cells over the envelope); within a stratum a flight is the first candidate that hides an antenna, so the strata weigh the speeds equally, not by how often each speed hides one.
- The speed floor of a level is a physics derivation from the measured bank and yaw-rate limits (no source measures the slowest shadowing event).
