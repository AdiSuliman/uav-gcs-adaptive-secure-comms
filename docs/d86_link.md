## Item 3 (physics-1, MAJOR). Airframe shadowing paired with Sun et al.'s fit by the burial, not the roll

**Why.** Sun et al.'s loss-against-roll fit (Part IV, Table II) comes from events at 1.4-2.9 deg elevation (Sec. II: "elevation angles between 1.4 and 2.9 degrees", largest rolls up to 58.3 deg), where the roll sets how deep the airframe lies in the line of sight. The model checked the level's roll band on the bank alone and gave the full depth at the bank, however far the GCS sat above the belly's horizon; our GCS lies up to about 23 deg below the UAV (the Eb/N0 distance 0.28-1.57 km, altitude 15-120 m). At 15 deg elevation, GCS abeam, a 16.1 deg bank buried the line of sight by 1.1 deg and took the whole 6-21 dB (knife-edge physics gives about 6 dB at such a grazing burial). The verifiers measured 24-41% of the counted frames (96% at 25 dB) below the smallest burial Sun et al.'s geometry gives at the band's lowest roll.

**Decision.** `shadow_equiv_roll.m` (new): the burial dl of an attitude, asind(-cz) of `uav_attitude_db.m` with the roll toward the GCS, and Sun et al.'s equivalent roll of that burial, the roll at which an aircraft in level flight at 2.9 deg elevation buries its line of sight as deep with the station abeam: req = dl + 2.9. 2.9 deg is the most severe of Sun et al.'s 1.4-2.9 deg (a given burial maps to the largest roll, so to the deepest level); `p.shadow_el_deg = 2.9`.
- **The event's pairing** (`shadow_frame.m`, `shadow_hidden.m`): an event is flown at the level when the equivalent roll of its deepest burial, the turn's bank with the GCS abeam, lies in the level's band (`shadow_roll_band.m`, unchanged band). In Sun et al.'s turns the station passed the beam (U-turns, Sec. III), where the roll less the elevation is the deepest burial of the event; their fit pairs the median loss with that largest roll. The check is one per event (bank, elevation and pitch only), so the level never switches on or off as the heading turns.
- **The depth within the event** (`shadow_frame.m`): Sun et al.'s shape (eq. 8, its rising half, as D85) on the burial's progress from the event's start (the belly's horizon, or the roll r_s where the heading's criterion came later) to its deepest. The whole depth lies only at the bank with the GCS abeam; a roll-in builds it up, and a held turn eases it as the GCS moves off the beam, toward the heading criterion or the tail.
- **The verifier's per-frame variant was implemented first and dropped**: checking the band on the equivalent roll at the frame's own heading made the level switch on mid-run as the heading turned (the pools' event-frame flights lost their onset at frame 11 in `test_shadow_onset_ramp`, and a band crossing during a roll-in gave a step onset, the artefact the D85 onset-shape cue must not learn), and with the GCS within a few degrees of the tail it paired a 1 deg burial with Sun et al.'s 40-53 deg rolls. The per-event pairing at the deepest burial has neither.
- **Fixed views** (`shadow_roll_band.m` second output, read by the survivability map, the gallery and the console): the bank whose equivalent roll is the roll at which the fit equals the level, the GCS abeam on the horizon (no altitude): the fit's roll less 2.9 deg (6-12 dB 13.2 deg, 15.5 dB 25.6, 20 dB 43.6, 25 dB 55.0; D85: 16.1, 28.5, 46.5, 57.9). An optional elevation argument gives the bank at another elevation. At the clamped levels (up to 12.4 dB on the band's lowest roll, 16.1 deg; 22.85 dB and up on its highest, 57.9 deg) the view's equivalent roll is the band's edge itself, so `shadow_frame.m` and `shadow_hidden.m` hold the band to 1e-6 deg: the rounding of the burial's arcsine, and of the model's parameters, which `link_seed.m` ('Att') and `build_threat_model.m` (the band) write to 10 digits. Without it the 22.85-25 dB views at 2 deg elevation, and the 25 dB view with a bank limit of 57.6 deg, fell 1.4e-14 and 7.1e-15 deg outside the band and hid no antenna. 1e-6 deg is far below any attitude difference the model resolves.
- **The survivability map's views over their run.** The depth follows the burial, and the coordinated turn carries the GCS off the beam during a run: at 72 km/h and 55.0 deg of bank the heading turns 40 deg/s, 45 deg over the map's 57 frames. With the GCS abeam at the first frame, the 22.5-25 dB cells eased to 19.2-20.8 dB by the run's end, below their labels and onto the 20 dB cell. The map flies its shadowing views with the GCS passing the beam at mid-run, as Sun et al.'s U-turns passed it (Sec. III): one line in `map_survivability_boundary.m`, after the bank is set, puts the GCS direction at the first frame half the run's heading change before the beam, g tan(bank) / max(v, `p.turn_v_floor`) (the coordinated turn `flight_draws.m` flies) times (RUN_FRAMES - 1) cycles / 2. The interferer's direction, set relative to the GCS's on the next line, follows; the other entries fly no bank, so nothing moves for them. Per level, the loss at the first and the last frame, its mean and its minimum over the run (dB), the GCS abeam at the first frame against the beam at mid-run: 15.5 dB 15.50, 15.47, 15.49, 15.47 against 15.50 throughout; 20 dB 20.00, 19.28, 19.84, 19.28 against 19.95, 19.95, 19.99, 19.95; 22.5 dB 22.50, 19.35, 21.76, 19.35 against 22.21, 22.21, 22.44, 22.21; 23 dB 23.00, 19.15, 22.08, 19.15 against 22.63, 22.63, 22.92, 22.63; 25 dB 25.00, 20.82, 24.00, 20.82 against 24.60, 24.60, 24.91, 24.60 (the GCS 22.5 deg before the beam at the first frame); 6-12 dB hold their level either way (3.7 deg of heading over half the run). ## Item 4 (physics-2, MAJOR). The emitter of opportunity is shadowed by the airframe

**Why.** The O4 reading (fault against shadowing, beside KPI 1) rested on the airframe hiding an antenna from the GCS's direction only; the emitter carried no airframe term and its direction was a broadside angle in [-90, 90] that hid its side. By the model's own rule an emitter above the belly's horizon on the banked side is hidden from the same antenna (the verifiers: about 20% of shadowing flights on the same antenna, about 40% on some antenna), which the O4 table assumed to be 0%.

**Decision.** `emitter_idle.m`:
- **Direction.** The emitter's azimuth is drawn uniform over the full circle, in the broadside convention of `gcs_aoa.m` (0 abeam on the right, 90 ahead), so it records the side; the draw keeps its place in the 'amb' stream (the third draw, the old broadside angle's), every other stream untouched.
- **Elevation** (`emitter_elevation.m`, new; a derivation): the farthest emitter that reaches the condition's INR, so the lowest in the UAV's sky and the most often hidden: our band at the licence-exempt density cap (ETSI EN 300 328 v2.2.2, 4.3.2.3: 10 dBm per MHz, `p.gcs_psd_dbm_mhz`, 11.0 dBm in our 1.25 MHz), free space at 2.4 GHz to the UAV's 2 dBi antenna, the floor kTB with the 5 dB noise figure (-108.0 dBm) plus the INR: 11.16, 3.53 and 1.12 km at INR 0, 10 and 20 dB; its antenna on a mast as the GCS's (10 m), the UAV at the flight's altitude, a flat earth (the earth's curvature would put it lower in the sky still): 0.56, 1.79 and 5.65 deg below a UAV at 120 m, 0.03-0.26 deg at 15 m; 0 without an altitude.
- **Shadow.** In every idle bin `shadow_frame.m` at the emitter's direction (turned with the heading), its elevation, the bin's roll (`turn_attitude.m`, a roll-in included), the flight's bank and pitch, the build's heading criterion and speed floor; its own horizon crossing starts the shape. The hidden antenna loses the share of the level's depth and its K is the shadow's once that loss counts as an event (item 13), exactly as the channel treats our signal. A direction the bank buries at its deepest at least as deep as the shallowest burial the level is paired with keeps the level (the emitter's band is [lo, Inf): a deeper burial keeps the level as a lower bound, Sun et al.'s fit rising with roll); only airframe-shadowing builds shadow it, as they alone shadow the GCS.
- **Its line of sight** takes the steering cos(el) sin(direction) and the Doppler phase of item 11 at fd cos(el).
- **Wording** (`emitter_reading.m`): the airframe hides an antenna from the directions above its belly's horizon; an emitter outside that region reaches the shadowed antenna like the others, one inside it is hidden too. The same sentence belongs in `docs/d85_obs.md` O4 ("hides an antenna from the GCS's direction only"), which this branch leaves to the merge. The O4 table comes from the A5 data of the full run.

**Numbers.** The A5 recipe's kept shadowing flights, an emitter at a uniform azimuth and its elevation at each INR, at the flight's last counted frame: hidden on the GCS's hidden antenna (its loss at least 5 dB) on 11.7% (6 dB), 15.2 (8), 15.8 (12), 17.8 (15.5), 18.2 (20), 16.8% (25 dB) of the draws; on some antenna 24.3-34.9%.

**Tests.** `test_idle_emitter_attenuation` rewritten: the faulty connector holds the emitter 18 dB down (unchanged); the elevation's distances (11.16, 3.53, 1.117 km) and angles at the flight's altitude, 0 without one; in a held 30 deg right bank an emitter drawn on the left (azimuth 120-240 deg) reaches the antennas within 6 dB of each other, one drawn on the right more than 30 deg off the nose is more than 7 dB down on the far end of the array (two seeds each, end to end through `emitter_idle.m` and `idle_rssi.m`).

## Item 10 (data-5). Every turning flight of every class rolls in

**Why.** Only shadowing flights rolled in, so the common change of the antenna gain toward the GCS and the heading-rate onset of a turn entry occurred only before shadowing events, a weak cue tied to one class (cm_slope, cm_sd) and a false-alarm case (turn entries without shadowing) that no test set held.

**Decision.** `flight_draws.m`: in the detector's sub-runs (`p.roll_in_frames` set: A5, the second test set, B4, B4s) every turning flight (heading-rate draw on, a bank above 0) rolls in from its own 'roll' stream, whatever its class. A shadowing build places its onset as before (the 5 dB crossing at the drawn frame, labels by the 5 dB rule). Any other build draws the roll-in's start uniform between T before the run's first frame and its last frame (T = bank / rate, the rate as `roll_rate.m` draws it from the first draw; the start from the third), so some of it lies inside the run; level flight before it, the held bank after it; the GCS direction drawn at the run's first frame; no antenna hidden, labels unchanged. No source gives the share of flight time spent rolling in, so every class gets the same rule (class symmetry, the verifier's requirement). The pools (`p.roll_in_frames` empty) keep their placement: the shadowing groups' flights and their clean reference roll in at the event frame, every other pool flight holds its bank; whether the other pool cells should roll in is a validation decision for the pools' owner. One-line hook outside this group's files: `run_dataset_sweep.m` sets `p0.roll_in_frames = p0.pl_onset_det` on the sweep's base params, after `p0.cap_frames` (the sweeper's and WLAN's hit shares are measured on p0 before it), so the clean class and every threat inherit it, as B4 and B4s set it on their base params (`eval_unseen_snr.m`, `eval_unseen_severity.m`). There it shares no line with d86-det's edits of the same file: `git merge-tree` of this branch with d86-det, d86-dec and d86-ops reports no conflict.

**The 2.5 dB note, corrected** (d85_onset.md "Stated, not measured" and Open risk 1: "under 2.5 dB at a 30 deg bank"): over 3000 clean dataset flights (speeds uniform, the Eb/N0 grid) the change of the antenna gain toward the GCS from level flight to the bank is p50 0.8, p90 4.6, p99 7.1, at most 11.9 dB (banks up to 57.9 deg, 38.6% above 30 deg); inside a 20-frame sub-run p90 1.0, p99 1.8 dB; inside a 12-cycle window p90 0.7, p99 1.3 dB (the verifier: 4.5 p90, 13.5 max; 0.7 and 1.4 in the window). A frame of the 20-frame run falls inside the roll-in on 98.3% of the turning clean flights.

**Open, for the main session to assign (detection or INT owner).** The verifier's corrected fix asks for the clean false-alarm rate, and the path-loss and shadowing false-alarm rates, on 'none' sub-runs during their roll-in, as a separate reading beside KPI 1; none of d86-det, d86-dec or d86-ops implements it, and it lies in `eval_detector.m`, outside this group's files. The selection: the test frames of the 'none' sub-runs (`dataset.class_run` 1) with `dataset.roll_in(:, 2) > 0` and `roll_in(:, 1) <= (pos - 1) * p.cycle_s <= roll_in(:, 1) + bank ./ roll_in(:, 2)` (`dataset.roll_in` columns: start [s] from the run's first frame, rate [deg/s]; `dataset.bank`, `dataset.pos`); reported there: the share labelled as any threat, as path_loss and as airframe_shadowing, next to KPI 1's clean false-alarm rate over all 'none' frames. Every turning clean sub-run carries a roll-in and 98.3% of them have a frame inside it, so the reading has its frames once A5 is rebuilt. Without it the class-symmetry change has no measured effect on false alarms; if it is not assigned, the D86 entry lists it as open.

**Tests.** New `test_roll_in_every_class` (clean, jamming, fault and path-loss builds, 120 flights each: every turning flight's roll-in is the 'roll' stream's rate and start, level before it, the bank after it, no antenna hidden; without `p.roll_in_frames` a held bank; `run_dataset_sweep.m` sets the roll-in on its base params before the clean class and the threats take them, and never clears it; the model's 'Att' block receives the roll-in).

## Item 11 (physics-3). The interferers' line-of-sight Doppler turns with the heading, its phase integrated

**Why.** Every interferer's steering and lever-arm phase turned with the heading, its LoS Doppler phase stayed 2 pi fd sin(aoa) t, the direction at the run's start (`build_threat_model.m`, `idle_rssi.m`, `emitter_idle.m`). The error is at most 2 fd sin(dpsi / 2): about 47 Hz over a 20-frame sub-run, 92 Hz over a 38-frame pool run, 139 Hz over the map's 57 frames at 358 Hz, where the heading-rate cap is g tan(57.9 deg) / v = 20 deg/s (the verifier's corrected figures; the finding's 132 and 197 Hz assumed 28.7 deg/s).

**Decision.** `los_phase.m` (new): 2 pi fd times the integral of sin(aoa + heading change) from 0 to t, the heading change of `turn_attitude.m`; in closed form for a constant or steadily turning heading, (cos(aoa) - cos(aoa + w t)) / w, and over a roll-in by the trapezoidal rule on 2001 points (phase error below 2e-3 rad at 358 Hz; 1.5e-5 rad measured). The threat block takes that phase at the frame's start plus the frame's own frequency, fd sin(aoa + dp) (t - t1), as A10 gives our LoS; `idle_rssi.m` and `emitter_idle.m` take the helper at the bin's centre (not sin(aoa + dq) tq, which the verifier showed adds a false frequency term). Every interferer's LoS phase is now continuous from frame to frame and over the idle time.

**For d84_link A10** (its Decision: "as the interferers take fd sin(aoa)"): "as each interferer takes fd sin(aoa + heading change), its line-of-sight phase integrated over the turn (`los_phase.m`; the threat block, `idle_rssi.m` and `emitter_idle.m`)."

**Tests.** New `test_los_phase_turns` (three roll-in timelines against a 1 us cumulative integral within 2e-3 rad; the held and the straight closed forms; the phase's rate equals fd sin(aoa + heading change) within 0.5 Hz before, during and after a roll-in; the block, `idle_rssi.m` and `emitter_idle.m` call it). `test_idle_rssi_quiet_slot` (the last idle bin against the frame's quiet slot: jamming, WLAN, tone, jamming + fault) and `test_onsets_in_the_model` pass with the block's draws replayed in the block's order (no draw moved).

## Item 12 (physics-4). Noise bursts: each flight one of the two measured patterns

**Why.** Every burst lasted U[0, 31] symbols and every sleep U[0, 31 (1 - a) / a] with a uniform in 0.2-0.775: below 50% the sleeps went beyond Xu et al.'s measured U[0, 31], and at 20% the pattern was not Marti et al.'s J4 (unit-symbol bursts) that the comments cited; 43% of the 32-symbol quiet slots held no jamming at 20% (0.08% under J4).

**Decision.** `jam_timing.m`, `build_threat_model.m`, `init_params.m`: the regime is drawn per flight by the 'jam' stream's fourth draw (no draw added): with probability `p.burst_j4(2)` = 0.5 Marti et al.'s J4 at its only measured duty, `p.burst_j4(1)` = 0.2: every one-symbol slot of the jammer's own clock (a random phase, so it stays unsynchronized to our symbols and frames) jammed with probability 0.2, runs of jammed slots 1 / (1 - 0.2) = 1.25 symbols on average; otherwise Xu et al.'s random jammer with the duty uniform in `p.burst_duty_range` = [0.5, 0.775]: bursts U[0, 31] symbols, sleeps U[0, 31 (1 - a) / a], at most 31 symbols, so 0.5 is their first jammer exactly and 0.775 their second's mean duty. No source gives how often each pattern is met: half each. The block tells the regimes apart by the duty (below Xu et al.'s range: J4). The first switch keeps its single draw, so `idle_rssi.m`'s replay of the block's draws is unchanged. Fixed geometries keep Xu et al.'s 50%.

**For d84_link A7** (its Decision and Tests): "Per flight the regime is drawn: Marti et al.'s J4 at 20% (one-symbol slots, each jammed with probability 0.2), or Xu et al.'s random jammer with the duty uniform in [0.5, 0.775] (bursts U[0, 31], sleeps U[0, 31 (1 - a) / a] <= 31 symbols); half the flights each. `test_noise_burst_duty`: the duty at 0.2, 0.5 and 0.775; at 0.2 runs of 1.25 symbols and almost no jam-free 32-symbol window; at 0.5 bursts of 15.5 symbols."

**Sources.** Xu et al. 2005 (A02), Sec. 2.1 and Table 1 (tj = U[0,31], ts = U[0,31] or U[1,8]); Marti et al. 2023 (V7_Marti2023_Mitigating_Smart_Jammers_MU_MIMO), Sec. VII-A (J4: "randomly selected bursts of unit length (i.e., one time slot)") and Fig. 5(d) (20%).

**Tests.** `test_noise_burst_duty` rewritten (anchor): in the model, the share on matches 0.2, 0.5 and 0.775; at 0.2 the mean run is 1.25 symbols (within 0.15) and under 1% of the 32-symbol windows are free of it; at 0.5 the mean burst 15.5 symbols (within 4) and under 5% free windows. `test_jam_timing` (half J4 at 0.2, the rest uniform in 0.5-0.775, the draw formula), `test_draws_independent` (the duty's uniform with no J4 share), `test_gated_jammers` (unchanged, Xu at 0.5), `test_slot_and_burst_measurements` (its flight's noise bursts set to Xu et al.'s 50%: the flight's own draw is now J4, whose one-symbol bursts, smeared by the delay line, keep 82-100% of a frame's 32-sample blocks busy and two of its eleven frames without a busy/idle edge, so "every frame shows edges" is a property of the random jammer, not of J4).
