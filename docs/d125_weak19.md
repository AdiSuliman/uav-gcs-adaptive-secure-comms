## D125 - Profile 3's three weak points of C2e/C2g (ROADMAP item 19): decision-time fixes of the deployed DQN, chosen on validation, read once on a fresh profile-3 test set (Adi's yes 18.11.2026: "Yes, fix them"; registered before any reading)

1. **What.** Three option fields of the DQN kinds, each off by default (`''` or absent). With all three off, every action, rung, memory field, stamp and printed number is the base commit's (a987d97) bit for bit (`test_weak19.m`: the parent's `policy_monitor.m`, `policy_decide.m`, `lb_complete.m`, `policy_stamp.m`, `decision_settings.m` and `pool_geometries.m` against this branch's, with the fields absent, `''` and `""`; the builder's off block evaluated from its source). None needs retraining: each acts after the agent's shield-masked argmax and the link-budget completion, before the escalation, on receiver measurements only (class, harm evidence, degradation, the configurations' known gains; no truth field). The rule kinds are untouched.

   | (a) Harmed lost-branch episodes: deployed DQN 32.9% antenna_fault, 5.9% airframe_shadowing; ungated rule 82.9%, 47.1%; DQN + escalation 82.9%, 17.6% | `PP.dqn_harm` (`q`, `rule`, `fec`); `dqn_fix.m` harm | A lost-branch class with harm evidence (`M.harm`: degraded, degraded without RF evidence, or the drop alarm, in the confirmation's cycles; the D121 gate's own reading), held at no_action by the DQN for C.esc confirmed cycles, takes one step: the highest-Q configuration not tried that holds a link-budget or space element (`q`; a hop alone restores no branch), the rule's lost-branch answer, power_control (spatial_diversity beside it where the predicted MMSE gain reaches the nulling threshold) (`rule`), or that answer with fec_interleave (`fec`).    **Keep.** With any layer on, a configuration a layer took is kept while its cause lasts (`dqn_fix.m` keep, `info.dk`): the lost-branch class (harm), a jamming class (hop), the drop alarm (step). A DQN proposal that gives the answer back is replaced by the configuration in use: no_action or a configuration with no link-budget or space element (harm), no_action or a hop (hop), a lower known gain (step). Any other proposal passes. The mark is cleared when the cause ends or another configuration is in use. Harm evidence is not asked for while keeping: the answer that works removes the degradation the evidence counts, and a release would bring back the loss and a second change (an excursion of two changes for no gain, against KPI 6). A completion action a layer replaced is not marked `info.lb`.

   Every candidate is one of the 36 configurations, so the pools serve them unchanged, inside the sources' measured ranges. `lb_more` needs the completion (`lb_complete` true, as deployed), and the stamp carries it only then. The stamp (`policy_stamp.m`) carries a field only when on, canonical (`step+fec` = `fec+step`), so every earlier stamp stays. The reviewer's second candidate for (c), single path_loss episodes at every severity in every retraining, already holds: `dqn_episode_spec.m` draws every cell of the split, every severity of every single threat included, and C2g removes only the left-out combination's pools. An oversampling weight would need retraining, so it is not registered (experiment only, not implemented).

   **Claim of (c).** The selection and the fresh read use the deployed agent, which trained on every combination. They measure the trained path-loss family and tone_jamming+path_loss only. The never-trained figure (87.1%) is not read again here: a never-trained claim needs C2g's 14 retrainings read on the fresh pools, a separate experiment and a separate decision.

2. **Selection, on the validation split only** (`weak19_reading.m` 'val'). The policy is the deployed DQN as deployed (its monitor, settings and the link-budget completion). The episodes are train_dqn.m's validation episodes (every cell, Eb/N0 and geometry twice, seed 99, frame draws 5000 + b). Beside them come a validation comb set: the jamming and reactive_jamming cells on every validation flight, twice, stream 99101, frame draws 5600 + b, the follower on the new channel at the hop and on frequency diversity's second carrier, as C2e's comb set. The clean validation flights are C2's 1800 (draws 41000 + b). The steps run in order, each on the setting the earlier ones adopted: step 1 `dqn_harm` (q, rule, fec against off), step 2 `dqn_hop` (q, rule), step 3 `lb_more` (fec, step, fec+step). A candidate is adopted over the setting in force only if:
   - (a) on its target (table, last column), recovered rises: the paired difference above 0 and its one-sided p-value below 0.05/k, k the step's number of candidates (Bonferroni: 3, 2, 3). With at least 30 clusters the p-value is the cluster bootstrap's (the share of resample means at or below 0); with fewer, the exact sign-flip test over the clusters' sums (each cluster's sum symmetric about 0 under no effect; Good 2005, ch. 3; exact by convolution on the integer sums). With fewer than 6 clusters the target cannot be chosen on (2^-6 is the smallest p-value that passes 0.05/3). Step 1's target is the harmed single antenna_fault episodes alone: the harmed combinations holding a lost branch (about 1,700 on validation, 94-98% recovered already) would swamp the about 64 harmed singles (p3.log: 72 harmed single lost-branch episodes in 10 clusters, 32 and 4 per repetition);
   - (b) no threat with at least 10 recoverable validation episodes, and not the comb set pooled, loses more than 1.0 point of recovered, nor 2.0 points at the lower bound. Step 1 adds two subgroups: the harmed single airframe_shadowing episodes (about 8, in 1-2 geometries: they cannot be chosen on, only guarded) and the harmed combinations holding a lost branch. Steps 2 and 3 add each target adopted before them. A subgroup with fewer than 30 clusters is judged on the point alone (no bound read; at these sizes -1.0 point means no net loss);
   - (c) pooled held and pooled recovered no lower than -0.5 point, lower bounds no lower than -1.0;
   - (d) KPI 6: the clean validation flights with a change stay within max(5%, the setting in force's) one-sided Clopper-Pearson bound, and at most 1 point above the setting in force's share (today 69 of 1800, bound 4.66%);
   - (e) the false changes per hour of flight of the deployed form on those flights stay within the registered limit, 470 (today 436.7). This is a point value, while (d) is a bound: up to about 10 more false changes on 1800 flights still pass (e). The asymmetry is registered as it is: a Garwood bound on today's 131 changes already lies above 470;
   - (f) the delivered payload after onset (`dlv_post`, the cost of rate_reduce's 4x lower rate): pooled no lower than -0.5 point, lower bound no lower than -1.0; the comb set as (b). Recovered can rise almost by construction under a 6 dB step; (f) keeps the rate that buys it in view.

   Bootstrap: clustered by flight (a geometry at an Eb/N0), 2000 draws, fixed seeds (1900 + k); sign-flip draws (non-integer sums only) from 1950 + seed. Among the candidates of a step that pass, the highest pooled held wins; within 0.5 point the larger target gain, then the higher return.

   **Reproduction first.** With every option off, the reading must reproduce the registered validation numbers it leans on, or it stops before any candidate and nothing is frozen:
   - the agent as selected: held and recovered (seed_summary.dqn_held, dqn_recovered);
   - the deployed form: clean flights with a change (lb.readings: 69 of 1800), and its false changes and their rate per hour (results/dqn_training.txt: 131, 436.7 per hour);
   - rule_sel: held, recovered and clean flights with a change.

   The gate of choose_deployed.m reads the agent as selected, before the completion and these layers, so the gate stays as it is. (d), (e) and (f) guard the deployed form's false alarms and rate.

   The selection is frozen with the SHA-256 of every .m file of this checkout and of the tree's inputs (`read_once.m`): results/weak19/<tag>/selection.json, val.mat and weak19_reading.txt. The validation marker is written last, in this checkout and in the tree (results/read_once/weak19_val.txt), with a copy of selection.json in the tree (results/read_once/weak19_selection.json). The val stage refuses when either marker exists or when the tree already holds the fresh pools (they are built only after a selection), so deleting the checkout or its results/ folder does not reopen the selection. Right after the val stage, selection.json is committed on weak19 with `git add -f` (results/ is ignored).

   Only the adopted setting is read, with the registered deployed DQN beside it as the baseline on the same episodes and frame draws (paired). The fresh sets are single, follower, combined (stream 2091) and comb (twice per flight).

   **Registered verdict.** One primary endpoint per adopted step: step 1 the harmed single lost-branch episodes (antenna_fault and airframe_shadowing; about 330 and 70 expected), step 2 the comb set, step 3 the path-loss family (trained). Each is a one-sided test of a gain (the same test as (a), at 0.05), with Holm across the adopted steps. Guards: pooled recovered and pooled held lower bounds at least -1.0 point, pooled delivered lower bound at least -1.0 point, the fresh KPI 6 Clopper-Pearson bound at most 5%, and at most 470 false changes per hour. The read prints CONFIRMED or NOT CONFIRMED per adopted step: confirmed when its endpoint passes Holm and every guard holds. Every other row is descriptive:
   - recovered, held and delivered, pooled;
   - the harmed lost-branch episodes, pooled and per threat, and the harmed combinations holding a lost branch;
   - the path-loss family and tone_jamming+path_loss;
   - every threat, the comb set and its delivered payload;
   - KPI 6 on the fresh clean flights (share, one-sided bound) and the false changes per hour.

   If nothing was adopted, the fresh set is not built and the test is not read.
