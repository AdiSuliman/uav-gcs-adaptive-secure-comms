## 1. Clean-pool swaps keep the split's event frames (integration-1, decision-1; BLOCKER)

**Why.** D85 gave the pools one event frame per geometry of every split (`PP.event_frame`, the rolling-in shadowing flights). Five places replace a split's runs with the 300 independent clean geometries of C1c (`train_dqn.m` clean_world, `evaluate_policies.m`, `edge_map.m`, `diag_false_alarms.m`, `diag_path_loss.m`) and kept the old event frames, so `link_env('tables')` indexed `K.ev{sp}(r)` beyond its end at the first clean-world call: C2 would stop at its first `eval_clean_wide` (after C1p/C1c), C2e and EDGE likewise. In the smoke shape (split smaller than the clean pool's 4 flights) nothing stopped, but clean flights 3-4 read event frame 11 (their tables skipped frames 1-10, the episodes started at 11 - 1 - onset).

**Decision.**
1. `clean_split.m` (new): the pools with split sp replaced by the clean pool CT. Every per-geometry field of the split follows CT's runs: `runs{sp}`, `event_frame{sp} = NaN(1, n)` (the clean geometries roll into no turn, build_clean_test_pools.m draws no roll-in), `speed{sp}`, the per-flight draws `cov{sp}` (CT.cov, flight_cov.m) with `alt{sp}` and `ksig{sp}`, the clean cell's `cell_geo` and no reference-only geometry (`ref_geo{sp}` empty); a field CT does not carry is left empty, never the replaced geometries'. The five sites call it (edge_map.m then empties the other splits, as before).
2. `link_env.m` tables: one event frame per geometry of every split or an error (`link_env:event_frame`) naming the split, so a stale per-geometry field can never be read silently again.
3. `train_dqn.m` clean_world caches the whole replaced split (pools, runs, event frames, speeds, cell_geo, cov, alt, ksig, ref_geo) and its tables; PPw carries the same fields.

**Tests.** test_clean_split_event_frames: a split of 3 geometries holding two rolling-in flights (event frame 11) replaced by 7 clean ones; the tables build with the 'mean' and the 'flight' reference, the episodes on geometries 4-7 have no event and read the clean pool's own runs; runs replaced without their event frames raise `link_env:event_frame`; the five sites call clean_split.m and none swaps `runs{2}`/`runs{TEST}` by hand.

## 4. The fusion window reads no step at our own configuration change (detector-02)

**Why.** D85's level-step cues (window_cues.m: stp_t, stp_db, stp_age on the antennas' combined gain; stp_t_mon, stp_db_mon on sinr + iot) read the monitor's window as received. A configuration carries a known gain (D84: power_control +4.00 dB in the received field, rate_reduce +6.02 dB in the despread SINR, both +10.02 dB); D84 took it out of the drop memory only. A release to no_action, or a switch away from a gain configuration, put a 4-10 dB step into the fusion window for up to fuse_N cycles: the path-loss signature the cue was built for (d85_obs.md O2). Under the deployed 'class_drop' monitor this cannot raise an alarm (path_loss counts only with the compensated drop), but it biases the fused probabilities the agent, the rule and the escalation read.

**Decision.** `policy_monitor.m` fusion(): the window holds every frame's antenna gains less the power step and its SINR less the whole known gain of the configuration it was received with (`obs.cfg_link`). `config_gain_db.m` gives the power step as a second output: 10 log10 of the pools' power factor (`PP.pw`), or, for a PP that carries only the gains (console, latency), the gain of power_control for every configuration holding it. rate_reduce's processing gain is already out of gain_ant (extract_closed_loop_frames.m:246), so subtracting the whole gain there would remove it twice. est_margin, sinr_gap and the absolute features stay as measured. Within one configuration every statistic of the window is unchanged (each is invariant to a common shift in dB: steps, slopes, spreads, offsets between antennas, the moment K), so the A5 fusion fit and every window without a change read as before; the branch records (bch) are relative and stay as measured.

**Tests.** test_monitor_release_fusion: a fusion whose path_loss logit reads stp_db and stp_db_mon (2 per dB, -12), a clean flight with 0.3 dB noise; held 12 cycles and released, power_control, rate_reduce and rate_reduce+power_control give exactly no_action's fused probabilities and step cues (1e-9) and the class none throughout; a real 4 dB step behind each held configuration reads path_loss on the 4 cycles the window holds it, its step cues above 3 dB; without the compensation the release of rate_reduce+power_control reads path_loss. Existing test_monitor_known_gain and test_rule_budget_match unchanged and passing.

## 6. Drop alarm: the window-limited CUSUM candidate, chosen on validation (planned addition)

**Source.** Xie, Zou, Xie & Veeravalli 2021 (JSAIT survey, library `V7_Xie2021_Sequential_Change_Detection_Survey_JSAIT.pdf`): Page's CUSUM, eq. 2-3 and its recursion W_n = (W_{n-1} + l(X_n))^+; Theorem 1 (Lai's lower bound of the conditional detection delay, |log a| / D(f1||f0)) and CUSUM's first-order optimality at the threshold |log a| under Lorden's and Pollak's formulations. Xie, Moustakides & Xie 2022 (Window-Limited CUSUM, library `W04_Xie2022_Window_Limited_CUSUM.pdf`): the WLCUSUM statistic eq. 6 with the post-change parameter estimated from the w samples before the current one (eq. 7, the MLE); Remark 4, a Gaussian mean shift with a set of possible changes theta >= 0.5: the estimate is the window's mean held in the set; Lemma 6, the optimal window is of the order sqrt(log ARL) / KL (for our 30-cycle episodes at a 5% false-alarm rate, ARL about 600 cycles, and a 6 dB change at 2-3 dB per-cycle spread, 1-2 cycles; a 4 dB change at 3 dB, about 5).

**Statistic.** `drop_cusum.m`: per cycle, d = the monitor's reference less the cycle's compensated Eb/N0 estimate (D84), th = the mean of the last C.cusum_win = 3 drops held in the set of changes D = the envelope's path-loss levels (6..22 dB, decision_config.m), W = max(0, W + th (d - th/2)) (the Gaussian mean-shift log-likelihood ratio times the estimate's variance, which the threshold absorbs: W in dB^2). A drop below half the smallest level never grows W. W is bounded at twice the threshold (section 7, items 1 and 5): the alarm (W >= thr) clears on the same evidence in reverse. In the monitor (`policy_monitor.m`, alarm mode 'class_cusum'): as 'class_drop', the drop alarm raised when W reaches PP.drop_db; the reference (`drop_ref.m`) holds the cycles of an excursion of W above 0 and adds them once W returns to 0 without the drop alarm, drops them when it raised it (the pre-change level is kept while the evidence of a change builds up; with a rolling reference a 4 dB step would be absorbed before it is called; section 7, item 1). M.drop (the rule's link budget, the agent's state) stays the median-window drop.

**Choice on validation (C1d, `choose_drop_threshold.m`, before C2).** Both forms read the same compensated estimate with the monitor's reference, alone (`drop_alarm_seq.m`; test: it equals the monitor's own drop alarm cycle by cycle in both modes). False alarms: the independent clean validation geometries of C1c (`data/clean_val_pools.mat`), one 30-cycle episode per geometry and Eb/N0 from the KPI 1 threshold up from a random frame, as train_dqn.m reads them; the drop alarm is read through the monitor's confirmation (`confirm_alarm.m`, any confirmation of C2's monitors: C.confirm and CFG.monitors; section 7, item 4), and an episode with a confirmed drop alarm in some cycle is a false alarm; a threshold is admissible when the one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson bound is at most 5% (KPI 6's bound, so the drop alarm alone never spends it). Detection: every path_loss cell of the validation split (5 levels, every Eb/N0 and geometry, 4 episodes each, onset at cycle 3-10, the same flight's clean link before it, link_env.m), detected at the first confirmed alarm at or after the onset. Grids: the median window 2:0.5:10 dB, the CUSUM 1:0.5:10 cycles of a 6 dB step at full drift (18-180 dB^2). Rule (`drop_alarm_form.m`): each form at its most sensitive admissible threshold; the CUSUM when it detects more than one point more, or within one point with a shorter mean delay; otherwise the median window (also on a tie, without an admissible CUSUM threshold, or without clean validation flights). The chosen form is deployed at the threshold it was compared at: the CUSUM at its pick, the median window at its pick, or at the train pools' threshold (D50) when it has no admissible one (section 7, item 6). The choice, the thresholds and the readings are saved in `data/drop_threshold.mat` (drop_db the median window's deployed threshold, drop_db_train, drop_from, drop_alarm, cusum_db, THC, VAL, CF, EBNO_THR) and appended to `results/drop_threshold.txt` with a "Deployed:" line.

**Use.** `train_dqn.m` reads the form: the monitors' 'class_drop' (main.m CFG.monitors, unchanged) reads as the chosen form; with the CUSUM the drop grid is its validation threshold and the next more sensitive ones of its grid, as many as DROP_STEPS (the grid does not grow); `trained_dqn.mat` then carries alarm_mode 'class_cusum' and drop_db the CUSUM threshold, in the agent's monitor and in rule_sel, so every reader that copies alarm_mode and drop_db (evaluate_policies, the experiments, edge_map, measure_latency, the console, v3d, the diagnostics) runs the deployed form unchanged. `det_frames.m`: DET's drop alarm in the deployed form (the CUSUM over the read cycles against the lead-in's reference, its window from the lead-in's last 3 drops). `edge_map.m` overhead pass: with 'class_cusum' the read is whether the CUSUM alarm is raised along the pass at every speed of the band, wings level and banked at every bearing (`drop_alarm_any.m`, run exactly only over the cycles that can raise it; the passes of overhead_pass.m), and a lost tracker's lasting step reads when it exceeds half the smallest change; with 'class_drop' as before (win_drop_db). `rollout_policy.m` records the drop alarm at the first change (fc_dal) and `diag_false_alarms.m` attributes false switches by it instead of comparing the median drop with the threshold. The diagnostics print the threshold in the deployed form's unit (`drop_unit.m`: dB, or dB^2 for the CUSUM), and `diag_path_loss.m`'s sensitivity sweep takes, with the CUSUM, the trained threshold and the next two more sensitive ones of its validation grid (THC of drop_threshold.mat) instead of 4 and 3 dB.

**Smoke run (scratch copy, not results).** C1d on the worktree's smoke pools (2 train geometries, 4 clean validation geometries x 2 Eb/N0 = 8 clean episodes, 80 path-loss episodes): 3.3 s; no threshold of either form admissible (with 8 episodes the bound cannot go below 31%), so the median window was kept. Detection on the 80 episodes: the median 100% at 2 dB falling to 67.5% at 10 dB, the CUSUM 100% at every threshold with the mean delay rising from 0.1 to 3.9 cycles. The pass reading on 324 (altitude, distance, band) cells took 34-99 s per threshold. The choice is made by the full run's C1d (300 clean geometries per Eb/N0). After the section 7 repairs, the same scratch C1d (3.1 s) confirmed by 2/2, 3/3, 4/5 or 5/5: the median window 5/8 false alarms at 2 dB, 1/8 at 4-5 dB, 0/8 from 5.5 dB (detection 93.8% there), the CUSUM 2/8 to 72 dB^2 and 1/8 from 81 (detection 100%); still none admissible with 8 episodes, the median window deployed at the train-pool threshold 5.0 dB ("train pools").

**Tests.** test_drop_cusum_alarm: the statistic of noise-free 6 and 10 dB steps (18 per cycle; 42, 84, 128.4, 178.4), none for a 2.5 dB drop; the monitor's 'class_drop' and 'class_cusum' equal drop_alarm_seq.m on six sequences (noise, 4/6/10 dB steps, a ramp); a noise-free 4 dB step raises the CUSUM at 36 on its 6th cycle (the reference held) and the median window at 3 dB on its 2nd; drop_alarm_any.m equals the whole sequence's reading in both modes, a 0.02 dB-per-cycle ramp over 1000 cycles never raises the CUSUM, an 8 dB step does; the selection rule's five cases; train_dqn maps every monitor call to the chosen form; C1d saves the form; DET and the edge map read 'class_cusum'; the threshold's unit per form, diag_path_loss.m's CUSUM sweep and no diagnostic (and not train_dqn) printing the threshold as 'drop %.1f dB' or 'drop %s dB'; the median window deployed at its own pick. test_drop_cusum_bounded (section 7).

## 7. Repairs after the review of this branch

Two reviewers checked the group's changes and raised nine issues (two pairs describe the same defect). Each was checked against the code first.

**Items 1 and 5 (MINOR, MAJOR): the CUSUM did not clear when a drop ended. Fixed.** Verified: `drop_cusum.m` had no upper bound, so during an L dB drop W grew by about L^2/2 per cycle and fell by only about 18 per cycle after it (noise-free 22 dB, 30 cycles: never cleared). Measured a second, related defect while checking it: the reference took only cycles with W at 0, and a cycle with a low estimate makes W positive, so the reference kept the high cycles and crept upward on a noisy clean link (2.4 dB per-cycle spread, 2000 flights, threshold 72: +1.14 dB at cycle 30, +1.34 dB at 100, +1.85 dB at 300), which made the alarm latch after a drop even with a bound.
- Bound: `W = min(max(0, W0 + th (d - th/2)), 2 thr)`. Source: Page's recursion, Xie et al. 2021 eq. 2-3, applied to the opposite log-likelihood ratio th (th/2 - d) of the return to the reference: below the bound, 2 thr - W is exactly that recursion reflected at 0, and the alarm clears when it reaches thr, the same evidence that raised it. During a lasting drop of at least the smallest level its drift per cycle (-L^2/2) is at least as negative as the forward statistic's on a clean link (-18), so a lasting drop clears falsely no more readily than a clean link alarms. The statistic up to its first crossing is unchanged, so every first-alarm reading stands.
- The reviewers' bounds were not used (2.4 dB spread, threshold 72, 2000 flights, with the reference below): at thr (item 5) the alarm flickers inside a lasting drop (a 6 dB drop's alarm broken after its raise in 92% of flights and 3/3-confirmed in 65% of its cycles, against 6% and 99% at 2 thr; 10 dB: broken in 43%, against 0%), and at thr + 22^2/2 (item 1) it cleared a median 13 cycles after a 6 dB drop and 11 after a 10 dB one (2 thr: 3 and 1).
- Reference (`drop_ref.m`, shared by the monitor and drop_alarm_seq.m): Page's statistic is the log-likelihood ratio counted from the maximum-likelihood change point, the last cycle it was 0 (Xie et al. 2021, eq. 3 and the text after it). The cycles since then are held while W > 0; when W returns to 0 without the drop alarm they were before any change and join the reference; when it raised the alarm they are the change's and are dropped. A building change is still kept out of the reference (the 4 dB step case of section 6 unchanged).
- Applied in `policy_monitor.m` (cap = PP.drop_db, `mem.rp`, `mem.rx` beside `mem.ref`), `drop_alarm_seq.m` (so C1d, drop_alarm_any.m and the edge map's pass reading follow) and `det_frames.m` seq_cusum (DET; its reference is the lead-in's, fixed, so only the bound applies).
- Measured (synthetic links, 2000 flights, a 30-cycle drop after 30 clean cycles; old -> new, threshold 72): cycles to clear after the drop ends at 2.4 dB spread: 6 dB median 32 -> 3 (not cleared within 60 cycles 48.2% -> 5.7%), 10 dB 49 -> 1 (92.0% -> 3.6%), 22 dB never -> 0 (100% -> 0.1%); at 1 dB spread 6 dB 25 -> 3, 10 dB 55 -> 1, 22 dB never -> 0. Clean-link false alarms at 2.4 dB: 30 cycles any 13.3% -> 9.2% (2/2 11.9% -> 8.5%), 300 cycles any 53.4% -> 14.4%; at 36: 30 cycles 26.4% -> 17.2%, 300 cycles 89.1% -> 54.8%. Detection within the drop: 6 dB 99.9% -> 98.9% (the old reference's upward bias had made every drop look larger), 10 and 22 dB 100%. The alarm stays up through the drop (6 dB: 99.4% of its cycles; the median window at 4 dB 59.2%). The remaining flights that do not clear are those whose reference was set high in the first cycles (a false alarm before the drop: 8-9% at this spread); see Open items.
- Numbers that move on the next run: C1d's CUSUM readings (false alarms lower, see above), C2 and every reader of the deployed CUSUM monitor (only if C1d chooses it), DET of path-loss combinations under 'class_cusum' (a cell's frames after rare clears inside a lasting drop), the edge map's pass reading (first raises, through the reference rule).
- Tests: test_drop_cusum_bounded: the bounded statistic of a noise-free 10 dB step and its end (42, 72, 72, 72, 22, 0), equal to the unbounded one up to the crossing; drop_ref.m on an excursion ending without and with the alarm; the monitor equals drop_alarm_seq.m in both modes on a 30-cycle 10 dB loss in 1 dB noise, a 30-cycle 22 dB loss followed by a 6 dB step and a 6 dB loss: held through each loss, clear within 2 cycles of its end, the later step raised from the restored reference on its 2nd cycle (W 72, 72, 0, 0 at cycles 39-42); on a clean link at 2.4 dB spread (400 flights, threshold 72) the reference within 0.6 dB of the level at cycle 100 (the old rule: +1.3 dB). test_drop_cusum_alarm unchanged and passing (its sequences end with the drop on).
