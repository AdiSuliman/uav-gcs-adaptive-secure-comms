## 1. Clean-pool swaps keep the split's event frames (integration-1, decision-1; BLOCKER)

**Why.** D85 gave the pools one event frame per geometry of every split (`PP.event_frame`, the rolling-in shadowing flights). Five places replace a split's runs with the 300 independent clean geometries of C1c (`train_dqn.m` clean_world, `evaluate_policies.m`, `edge_map.m`, `diag_false_alarms.m`, `diag_path_loss.m`) and kept the old event frames, so `link_env('tables')` indexed `K.ev{sp}(r)` beyond its end at the first clean-world call: C2 would stop at its first `eval_clean_wide` (after C1p/C1c), C2e and EDGE likewise. In the smoke shape (split smaller than the clean pool's 4 flights) nothing stopped, but clean flights 3-4 read event frame 11 (their tables skipped frames 1-10, the episodes started at 11 - 1 - onset).

**Decision.**
1. `clean_split.m` (new): the pools with split sp replaced by the clean pool CT. Every per-geometry field of the split follows CT's runs: `runs{sp}`, `event_frame{sp} = NaN(1, n)` (the clean geometries roll into no turn, build_clean_test_pools.m draws no roll-in), `speed{sp}` and the clean cell's `cell_geo`. The five sites call it (edge_map.m then empties the other splits, as before).
2. `link_env.m` tables: one event frame per geometry of every split or an error (`link_env:event_frame`) naming the split, so a stale per-geometry field can never be read silently again.
3. `train_dqn.m` clean_world caches the whole replaced split (pools, runs, event frames, speeds, cell_geo) and its tables; PPw carries the same fields.

**Tests.** test_clean_split_event_frames: a split of 3 geometries holding two rolling-in flights (event frame 11) replaced by 7 clean ones; the tables build with the 'mean' and the 'flight' reference, the episodes on geometries 4-7 have no event and read the clean pool's own runs; runs replaced without their event frames raise `link_env:event_frame`; the five sites call clean_split.m and none swaps `runs{2}`/`runs{TEST}` by hand.

Checked on the worktree's smoke pools (pre-D85, an event frame added for the check): `clean_split` + `link_env('tables')` run, K.nR = [2 4 2 2 4], `K.ev{2}` all NaN; a hand swap without event frames stops with the error. The smoke C2/C2e/EDGE FA numbers read on mis-framed clean flights (d85 smoke) are superseded by the main session's smoke after the merge.

## 2. Every pool build keeps its finished cells on disk (ops-4, code part)

**Why.** C1p (about 73-107 h per profile, DECISIONS.md:770 with d84_shadow/d85 increments) held every cell's frames in the client until one save at the end; PROJECT_LOG.md:865 records a power loss during v6 C1p with nothing kept. C1c's clean pools (300 geometries x 2 sets) and check pools (68 688 simulations, d84_shadow.md) take hours too.

**Decision.** `pool_checkpoint.m` (new): `key` (SHA-256 of a MATLAB value, as detector_id.m), `todo` (the cells without a finished file of the same key; unfinished temporary files removed), `save` (the cell's result and key to `cell_NNNN.tmp.mat`, renamed once complete: a write cut off never looks finished), `load`, `commit` (the build's output written to `<name>.tmp.mat` and renamed) and `clear`.
- `build_policy_pools.m`: the key of a cell is {MODEL_TAG, DET_ID, the geometry ids of every split, the cell (threat, severity, level, splits, positions), EBNO, ACTIONS, its geometries' draws (geoc{c}: seeds, speeds, altitudes, K, AoA, rho, frames, event frames), the frame options, its params (threat_params)}. The parfor runs only over the missing cells; each worker writes its cell to `data/c1p_cells` (an absolute path: the workers work in their own folders, pool_worker_init.m) and returns nothing, so the client no longer accumulates the pools (ops-6 (c)); the pools are assembled one cell at a time from disk, `data/policy_pools.mat` is committed atomically and the cells are removed after it.
- `build_clean_test_pools.m`: the same per chunk of 10 geometries (`data/c1c_<set>_chunks`; key {model tag, detector, runs, EBNO, ACTIONS, the chunk's seeds and speeds, options, params}).
- `build_check_pools.m`: the same per cell (`data/c1c_check_cells`; key {model tag, detector, runs, cell, its level field, the check Eb/N0, ACTIONS, geoc{c}, options, params}).
- A resume after a crash inside C1p loses at most the cells in progress (up to 6, the longest the clean cell, about 22-32 h of one worker by the review's estimate); the launcher's RESUME mode is the LCH group's.
- Disk: while a build runs, its finished cells (about the size of the output, 12-15 GB for C1p at the full layout) sit beside an existing output file; the commit writes the new output beside the old one before the rename. Peak about 2-3 times the output for C1p, inside the 150 GB floor.

**Tests.** test_pool_checkpoint_resume: a tiny build (5 cells, a deterministic job) stopped by an error after cell 3, with cell 4's temporary file cut off, resumes computing cells 4 and 5 only and assembles exactly the uninterrupted build's result; the temporary file is gone; another detector's key recomputes that cell; `commit` writes the variables and leaves no temporary file; `clear` removes the folder; the three builders use todo/save/load/commit/clear, absolute folders, no direct save of their outputs; the key of C1p holds MODEL_TAG, DET_ID, runs, the cell, EBNO, ACTIONS, geoc and opt.

Checked end to end on the smoke configuration (scratch copies, single process): C1c's clean validation set built uninterrupted, and the same build killed (taskkill of its own process tree) after its first chunk was on disk, then started again: see "Resume check" below.

## 3. Memory: the splits' norm alone, C2 on its own splits, C1c without the pools' frames (ops-6)

**Why.** The pools are about 12.4 GB in memory at the full layout (40.2 M stored frames, review ops-6 and its verifier), and the profiles overlap (C1p of one profile beside C2-EDGE of another, P2's B1 beside P3's C1p): 32-40 GB against 31.2 GB of RAM. Three avoidable holders: the pool builders loaded the whole `data/splits.mat` (7 GB at full scale) for the z-score; C2 kept splits 3-5 it never reads; build_check_pools kept the whole PP through its parfor.

**Decision.**
1. `prepare_data.m` saves `norm` and `classes` also as top-level variables of `data/splits.mat` (`save -struct ... -append`, no workspace variable named norm); `split_norm.m` (new) reads only them (a file without them is read whole, so older files work). The four sites use it: build_policy_pools.m, build_clean_test_pools.m, build_check_pools.m and prepare_data.m's second test set.
2. `train_dqn.m`: right after its tables, `PP.pools(:, :, :, 3:end) = {[]}` (C2 reads splits 1-2 only: training, validation, policy_table on split 1; clean_world replaces split 2; the tables of splits 3-5 are built before the drop and never read).
3. `build_check_pools.m` copies the fields it uses (singles, ebno, actions, F_SUB, speed range, model tag, det_id) and clears PP before simulating.

**Numbers.** Smoke pools of the worktree: PP 65.1 MB in memory, 29.4 MB without splits 3-5; the smoke `splits.mat` 365.8 MB loaded whole against 4 kB for norm and classes. Full layout (pool_layout.m with build_policy_pools.m's grid, 6 Eb/N0 x 36 configurations, 20 frames per flight and 38 per rolling-in flight): geometries per split [40 30 60 48 372], stored frames [3.91 2.93 5.87 5.48 22.05] M; splits 1-2 are 17.0% of the frames: at about 309 B per frame C2's resident PP falls from 12.44 GB to 2.12 GB. Measured on the smoke configuration (Windows process counters, one fresh MATLAB process per reading): reading norm and classes with split_norm.m leaves the process's peak working set at 1159 MB, reading the whole smoke `splits.mat` (as the builders did) raises it from 1157 to 1426 MB (+269 MB; at full scale about 7 GB). Dropping splits 3-5 takes C2's PP from 62.1 to 28.0 MB (whos), but the process's private bytes stay at 1049 MB: the smoke pools are many arrays of a few kB, whose blocks MATLAB's heap keeps for its next allocations (clearing the whole PP does not lower them either; reloading it then adds 12 MB of 62), so on the smoke pools the drop lowers what C2 needs next, not the counters. Arrays of 0.1 MB and more return to the system when freed (10 000 x 0.1, 833 x 1.2 and 100 x 10 MB: private bytes back to the start each time); at the full layout one pool field of split 5 is about 7 440 frames (372 geometries x 20) x 40 features in single, about 1.2 MB.

**Tests.** test_split_norm_memory (an old-style and a new-style splits file give the same norm and classes; prepare_data writes them; no builder loads `splits.mat` whole; train_dqn drops splits 3-5 right after its tables; build_check_pools clears PP).

## 4. The fusion window reads no step at our own configuration change (detector-02)

**Why.** D85's level-step cues (window_cues.m: stp_t, stp_db, stp_age on the antennas' combined gain; stp_t_mon, stp_db_mon on sinr + iot) read the monitor's window as received. A configuration carries a known gain (D84: power_control +4.00 dB in the received field, rate_reduce +6.02 dB in the despread SINR, both +10.02 dB); D84 took it out of the drop memory only. A release to no_action, or a switch away from a gain configuration, put a 4-10 dB step into the fusion window for up to fuse_N cycles: the path-loss signature the cue was built for (d85_obs.md O2). Under the deployed 'class_drop' monitor this cannot raise an alarm (path_loss counts only with the compensated drop), but it biases the fused probabilities the agent, the rule and the escalation read.

**Decision.** `policy_monitor.m` fusion(): the window holds every frame's antenna gains less the power step and its SINR less the whole known gain of the configuration it was received with (`obs.cfg_link`). `config_gain_db.m` gives the power step as a second output: 10 log10 of the pools' power factor (`PP.pw`), or, for a PP that carries only the gains (console, latency), the gain of power_control for every configuration holding it. rate_reduce's processing gain is already out of gain_ant (extract_closed_loop_frames.m:246), so subtracting the whole gain there would remove it twice. est_margin, sinr_gap and the absolute features stay as measured. Within one configuration every statistic of the window is unchanged (each is invariant to a common shift in dB: steps, slopes, spreads, offsets between antennas, the moment K), so the A5 fusion fit and every window without a change read as before; the branch records (bch) are relative and stay as measured.

**Tests.** test_monitor_release_fusion: a fusion whose path_loss logit reads stp_db and stp_db_mon (2 per dB, -12), a clean flight with 0.3 dB noise; held 12 cycles and released, power_control, rate_reduce and rate_reduce+power_control give exactly no_action's fused probabilities and step cues (1e-9) and the class none throughout; a real 4 dB step behind each held configuration reads path_loss on the 4 cycles the window holds it, its step cues above 3 dB; without the compensation the release of rate_reduce+power_control reads path_loss. Existing test_monitor_known_gain and test_rule_budget_match unchanged and passing.

## 5. EDGE reads rolling-in flights from their event frame (integration-2, decision-2)

**Why.** link_env reads a rolling-in flight (D85, the shadowing groups' 38-frame flights, event frame 11) from the event's frame after the same flight's clean link as lead-in; the K tables, the 'flight' clean reference and PP.mber/mfer use event_rows. Two edge-map readers did not: LINK accumulated the clean pool's packet loss over all 38 frames of the reference flights (the LINK_T target max(2 x clean, 1.4%) and the veto band's tl), and det_frames counted every frame of the three combinations with airframe shadowing (frames 1-10: the jammer, tone or spoofer alone, 26% of each flight's frames, never read by an episode), and for '+path_loss' took the drop reference from the clean flight's frames 29-38 (late in the turn, up to 2.5 dB of common gain change, d85_onset.md Open risk 1).

**Decision.**
1. `edge_map.m`: LINK of the clean pool (FT) and of the check pools' clean cell (KF) over `event_rows` (the tables' event frames `K.ev{sp}`, `Kc.ev{1}`): only the reference rows of rolling-in flights change; the clean link's own flights (NaN events), CL, CB, the check points' LINK and every FA claim are unchanged.
2. `det_frames.m` takes the split's geometry ids and event frames (edge_map.m passes `PP.runs{sp}, K.ev{sp}` and `CK.runs{1}, Kc.ev{1}`): a run with a finite event frame e is read as its episodes read it, the same flight's clean rows 1..e-1 as the lead-in and the cell's rows e..end, for every cell joined or not; the cell's rows 1..e-1 are not read (on false, age NaN). The fusion window, the latch and the drop reference (the median of the lead-in's last 10 cycles) follow from the joined rows. Runs without an event frame read as before.

**Tests.** test_det_frames_rolling_in: a 'jamming+airframe_shadowing' flight (jammer from frame 1, event frame 11) with a fusion that reads the window's mean log-probabilities: frames 1-10 not counted, age from frame 11, the fused class at frame 11 is the clean lead-in's (read from the run's first frame instead, as before: jamming); '+path_loss' with the clean flight at 10 dB before the event and 30 dB after it: no drop alarm on a flight at 10 dB (its reference the lead-in, not frames 29-38), a 6 dB drop raised from frame 12, for 'class_drop' and 'class_cusum'. test_det_frames_onset and test_det_frames_drop unchanged and passing. The edge map is under the hash rule: its LINK_T targets and DET/DET_h of the three combinations with airframe shadowing move on the next EDGE.

## 6. Drop alarm: the window-limited CUSUM candidate, chosen on validation (planned addition)

**Source.** Xie, Zou, Xie & Veeravalli 2021 (JSAIT survey, library `V7_Xie2021_Sequential_Change_Detection_Survey_JSAIT.pdf`): Page's CUSUM, eq. 2-3 and its recursion W_n = (W_{n-1} + l(X_n))^+; Theorem 1 (Lai's lower bound of the conditional detection delay, |log a| / D(f1||f0)) and CUSUM's first-order optimality at the threshold |log a| under Lorden's and Pollak's formulations. Xie, Moustakides & Xie 2022 (Window-Limited CUSUM, library `W04_Xie2022_Window_Limited_CUSUM.pdf`): the WLCUSUM statistic eq. 6 with the post-change parameter estimated from the w samples before the current one (eq. 7, the MLE); Remark 4, a Gaussian mean shift with a set of possible changes theta >= 0.5: the estimate is the window's mean held in the set; Lemma 6, the optimal window is of the order sqrt(log ARL) / KL (for our 30-cycle episodes at a 5% false-alarm rate, ARL about 600 cycles, and a 6 dB change at 2-3 dB per-cycle spread, 1-2 cycles; a 4 dB change at 3 dB, about 5).

**Statistic.** `drop_cusum.m`: per cycle, d = the monitor's reference less the cycle's compensated Eb/N0 estimate (D84), th = the mean of the last C.cusum_win = 3 drops held in the set of changes D = the envelope's path-loss levels (6..22 dB, decision_config.m), W = max(0, W + th (d - th/2)) (the Gaussian mean-shift log-likelihood ratio times the estimate's variance, which the threshold absorbs: W in dB^2). A drop below half the smallest level never grows W. In the monitor (`policy_monitor.m`, alarm mode 'class_cusum'): as 'class_drop', the drop alarm raised when W reaches PP.drop_db; the reference takes only cycles without an alarm and with W at 0 (the pre-change level is kept while the evidence of a change builds up; with a rolling reference a 4 dB step would be absorbed before it is called). M.drop (the rule's link budget, the agent's state) stays the median-window drop.

**Choice on validation (C1d, `choose_drop_threshold.m`, before C2).** Both forms read the same compensated estimate with the monitor's reference, alone (`drop_alarm_seq.m`; test: it equals the monitor's own drop alarm cycle by cycle in both modes). False alarms: the independent clean validation geometries of C1c (`data/clean_val_pools.mat`), one 30-cycle episode per geometry and Eb/N0 from the KPI 1 threshold up from a random frame, as train_dqn.m reads them; an episode with the drop alarm in some cycle is a false alarm; a threshold is admissible when the one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson bound is at most 5% (KPI 6's bound, so the drop alarm alone never spends it). Detection: every path_loss cell of the validation split (5 levels, every Eb/N0 and geometry, 4 episodes each, onset at cycle 3-10, the same flight's clean link before it, link_env.m). Grids: the median window 2:0.5:10 dB, the CUSUM 1:0.5:10 cycles of a 6 dB step at full drift (18-180 dB^2). Rule (`drop_alarm_form.m`): each form at its most sensitive admissible threshold; the CUSUM when it detects more than one point more, or within one point with a shorter mean delay; otherwise the median window (also on a tie, without an admissible CUSUM threshold, or without clean validation flights). The choice, the CUSUM threshold and the readings are saved in `data/drop_threshold.mat` (drop_alarm, cusum_db, THC, VAL, EBNO_THR) and appended to `results/drop_threshold.txt`. The median window's own threshold stays the train pools' (D50).

**Use.** `train_dqn.m` reads the form: the monitors' 'class_drop' (main.m CFG.monitors, unchanged) reads as the chosen form; with the CUSUM the drop grid is its validation threshold and the next more sensitive ones of its grid, as many as DROP_STEPS (the grid does not grow); `trained_dqn.mat` then carries alarm_mode 'class_cusum' and drop_db the CUSUM threshold, in the agent's monitor and in rule_sel, so every reader that copies alarm_mode and drop_db (evaluate_policies, the experiments, edge_map, measure_latency, the console, v3d, the diagnostics) runs the deployed form unchanged. `det_frames.m`: DET's drop alarm in the deployed form (the CUSUM over the read cycles against the lead-in's reference, its window from the lead-in's last 3 drops). `edge_map.m` overhead pass: with 'class_cusum' the read is whether the CUSUM alarm is raised along the pass at every speed of the band, wings level and banked at every bearing (`drop_alarm_any.m`, run exactly only over the cycles that can raise it; the passes of overhead_pass.m), and a lost tracker's lasting step reads when it exceeds half the smallest change; with 'class_drop' as before (win_drop_db). `rollout_policy.m` records the drop alarm at the first change (fc_dal) and `diag_false_alarms.m` attributes false switches by it instead of comparing the median drop with the threshold. The diagnostics print the threshold in the deployed form's unit (`drop_unit.m`: dB, or dB^2 for the CUSUM), and `diag_path_loss.m`'s sensitivity sweep takes, with the CUSUM, the trained threshold and the next two more sensitive ones of its validation grid (THC of drop_threshold.mat) instead of 4 and 3 dB.

**Smoke run (scratch copy, not results).** C1d on the worktree's smoke pools (2 train geometries, 4 clean validation geometries x 2 Eb/N0 = 8 clean episodes, 80 path-loss episodes): 3.3 s; no threshold of either form admissible (with 8 episodes the bound cannot go below 31%), so the median window was kept. Detection on the 80 episodes: the median 100% at 2 dB falling to 67.5% at 10 dB, the CUSUM 100% at every threshold with the mean delay rising from 0.1 to 3.9 cycles. The pass reading on 324 (altitude, distance, band) cells took 34-99 s per threshold. The choice is made by the full run's C1d (300 clean geometries per Eb/N0).

**Tests.** test_drop_cusum_alarm: the statistic of noise-free 6 and 10 dB steps (18 per cycle; 42, 84, 128.4, 178.4), none for a 2.5 dB drop; the monitor's 'class_drop' and 'class_cusum' equal drop_alarm_seq.m on six sequences (noise, 4/6/10 dB steps, a ramp); a noise-free 4 dB step raises the CUSUM at 36 on its 6th cycle (the reference held) and the median window at 3 dB on its 2nd; drop_alarm_any.m equals the whole sequence's reading in both modes, a 0.02 dB-per-cycle ramp over 1000 cycles never raises the CUSUM, an 8 dB step does; the selection rule's five cases; train_dqn maps every monitor call to the chosen form; C1d saves the form; DET and the edge map read 'class_cusum'; the threshold's unit per form, diag_path_loss.m's CUSUM sweep and no diagnostic printing the CUSUM threshold in dB.
